📖 Investment Style Guide - Fund Classification
GARP (Growth at Reasonable Price)

Buy growth stocks but at fair valuations. Balanced approach between growth and value.

Momentum

Buy stocks with strong price momentum. High turnover, volatile but can outperform in bull markets.

Quality Growth

Focus on high ROE, clean balance sheets, consistent earnings growth. Lower risk.

Value/Contra

Buy out-of-favor stocks trading below intrinsic value. Requires patience, contrarian bets.

Passive/Index

Track market indexes. Low cost, no stock picking, broad market exposure.

Blend

Mix of growth and value. Flexible mandate, manager discretion.

Sectoral/Thematic

Concentrated bet on single sector. High risk-reward, requires timing.

Liquid/Debt

Capital preservation, ~7% returns. Emergency fund, parking cash.
