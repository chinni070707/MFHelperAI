Quick Comparison

Option Effort Visual Impact Performance Dependencies
CSS-Enhanced Emojis Low (~30 min) Good Excellent None
SVG Icons Medium (~1-2 hrs) Good Excellent ~20KB icons
3D Icon Images ⭐ Medium (~1-2 hrs) Excellent Good ~200KB images
Lottie Animations High (~3-4 hrs) Excellent++ Good ~50KB library + animations

Recommendation: Option 3 (3D Icon Images) provides the best balance of visual impact and implementation effort. It's what modern banking apps like K Bank, HanaBank, and N26 use.
