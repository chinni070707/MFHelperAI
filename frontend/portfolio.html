💡 Need Help?

Where to get CAS?

Download from CAMS (camsonline.com) or KFintech (kfintech.com) websites. It's free!

Is my data safe?

Yes! All data is processed securely. We never store your CAS files permanently.

Can I edit later?

Absolutely! You can add, edit, or remove holdings anytime from the dashboard.
