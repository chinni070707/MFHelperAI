# Getting Started with CAS Upload

Managing your mutual fund portfolio just got easier! MFHelper's CAS (Consolidated Account Statement) upload feature allows you to import your entire portfolio in seconds. No manual entry required.

## What is a CAS Statement?

A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) is a single document that contains all your mutual fund investments across different Asset Management Companies (AMCs). In India, CAS statements are issued by:

- **CAMS (Computer Age Management Services)** - Covers most AMCs
- **KFintech (Karvy/KFinTech)** - Covers remaining AMCs

You receive CAS statements monthly via email from these registrars.

## How to Upload Your CAS to MFHelper

### Step

 1: Get Your CAS Statement

1. Check your email inbox for "Consolidated Account Statement" 
2. Look for emails from `cams@camsonline.com` or `kfintech@...`
3. Download the PDF attachment (usually password-protected)

### Step 2: Upload to MFHelper

1. Log in to your MFHelper account
2. Navigate to **Dashboard** → **Upload CAS**
3. Select your CAS PDF file
4. Enter the password (usually your DOB in DDMMYYYY format or PAN)
5. Click **Upload & Analyze**

That's it! Within seconds, MFHelper will:

- ✅ Parse all your mutual fund holdings
- ✅ Calculate current values and returns
- ✅ Analyze portfolio allocation
- ✅ Identify overlaps between funds
- ✅ Generate personalized insights

## What Data Does MFHelper Extract?

From your CAS statement, we extract:

- **Fund Details**: Scheme names, AMC, category
- **Holdings**: Units held, NAV, current value
- **Transactions**: Purchase dates, SIPs, redemptions
- **Returns**: Absolute returns, XIRR calculations
- **Folio Numbers**: For tracking across statements

## Security & Privacy

Your financial data security is our top priority:

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "Invalid Password" Error

- Most CAS files use DOB (DDMMYYYY) or PAN as password
- Try variations: DDMMYYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, PAN number
- Check email body for custom password

### "File Not Supported" Error

- Ensure you're uploading the **original PDF** from CAMS/KFintech
- Screenshots or converted files won't work
- File should be under 5MB

### Missing Funds

- Some older funds may not be in our database yet
- We're constantly updating our fund master data
- Report missing funds via the feedback form
